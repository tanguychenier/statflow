Core capabilities

Everything you need to understand your users

Lumen captures the full picture of how visitors interact with your product — with zero impact on their privacy.

Pageview Analytics

Real-time and historical pageview data broken down by page, referrer, country, device, and custom dimensions — all without cookies.

Heatmaps & Click Maps

Visualise exactly where users click, tap, and scroll. Identify cold spots and friction in any page layout at a glance.

User Journeys

Map the full path users take through your product. Spot drop-offs, loops, and unexpected routes in an intuitive Sankey diagram.

Conversion Funnels

Define multi-step funnels and immediately see where users fall off — by segment, device, or traffic source.

Custom Events

Track any interaction with a single line of JS — button clicks, form submissions, video plays — and query them in real time.

Open Data API

Pull raw events or aggregated metrics directly from ClickHouse via the REST or SQL API. Your data, your infrastructure.
