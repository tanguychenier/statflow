FAQ

Common questions

What counts as an "event"?
Every hit sent to Lumen — a pageview, a custom event, a click, or a form submission — counts as one event. Heatmap recordings count as a single event per session regardless of how many interactions are captured.
Can I self-host Lumen instead of using the cloud?
Yes — Lumen is fully open-source under the MIT licence. You can run the entire stack (tracker, ingestion API, ClickHouse, and dashboard) on your own infrastructure. Pricing only applies to our managed cloud offering.
Do I really not need a cookie banner?
In the vast majority of jurisdictions, yes. Lumen does not set cookies, does not fingerprint devices, and does not share data with third parties. We recommend confirming with your legal team for your specific situation, but most Lumen customers operate without a consent banner.
What happens if I exceed my plan's event limit?
We'll notify you by email when you reach 80% and 100% of your monthly limit. Events over the limit are queued and processed when you upgrade — we never silently drop data.
Is there a free trial for paid plans?
Growth comes with a 14-day free trial — no credit card required. Enterprise customers get a 30-day proof-of-concept period on dedicated infrastructure.
