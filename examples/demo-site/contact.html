Quick answers

How fast is the response time?

We aim to reply within one business day for all enquiries. Enterprise customers get a 4-hour SLA during business hours.

Is there a public issue tracker?

Yes — GitHub Issues is the primary venue for bug reports and feature requests. The contact form routes to the same team.

Can I book a live demo?

Absolutely. Select "Sales / Enterprise inquiry" in the form and mention you'd like a demo call — we'll send a calendar link.
